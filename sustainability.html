Sustainability

Sustainability Commitment

At Lains Dzhamp LLP, we proactively take steps to improve the sustainability of our refinery and logistics assets. We are taking the grit we have as an industry and applying it to more efficient, cleaner, and sustainable energy sources.

Emission Reductions

Implementing advanced filtration and capturing technologies.

Resource Optimization

Maximizing water recycling and energy recovery across our facilities.

Lains Dzhamp LLP is committed to producing petroleum products in a manner that is protective of the health and safety of our employees, customers, contractors and neighbors as well as in a manner that is environmentally responsible while fully meeting and/or exceeding applicable federal, state local laws & regulations.
